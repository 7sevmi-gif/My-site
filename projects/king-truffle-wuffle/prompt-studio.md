# Studio-grade video prompt: King Truffle Wuffle vs. the Manananggal

Model: Seedance 2.5 (or Kling 3 / Veo 3.1). Attach the references in this order:
`@image1` = refs/king.png, `@image2` = refs/manananggal.png, `@image3` = refs/jungle.png.
Paste everything below the line as one prompt.

---

STYLE: High-budget 3D animated feature film, theatrical-release quality. Stylized
cartoon characters with appealing chunky proportions, hand-sculpted forms and
painterly textures with visible brushwork on surfaces; no photoreal CGI, no
plastic look. Full feature-animation principles: strong anticipation, squash and
stretch, overshoot and settle, overlapping action and follow-through on cloth,
moss, hair and wings; key poses held for readability, comedic timing with
snappy accents. The camera is an invisible character moving through true 3D
space with deep parallax and foreground occlusion from ferns, vines and
mushrooms. Palette: deep green jungle dusk, warm earthy umbers and moss greens,
glowing amber mushroom light, with saturated violet and orange as the only
accents (the Manananggal). Mood: eerie suspense that snaps into heroic slapstick
triumph. Aspect 16:9, 15s, 3 motivated cuts.

LEGEND
@image1 = KING (King Truffle Wuffle: stout mushroom-gnome king, dark truffle cap,
grey beard, tilted golden crown, moss cape, bark armor, twig sword)
@image2 = MANANANGGAL (flying vampire witch, bat wings, glowing orange eyes,
fangs, long tongue, upper body only, trailing purple mist)
@image3 = CLEARING (Duwende kingdom: giant roots, glowing mushrooms, tiny
mushroom houses, muddy path, fireflies)
Maintain exact appearance from the reference images; consistent characters, no
deformation, no drift, no extra limbs.

Shot 1 (0–5s): A slow, low, ground-skimming push through the CLEARING at dusk,
inches above the mud, fern fronds and glowing mushroom stems sliding past the
lens as foreground occlusion. Fireflies drift. The glowing mushrooms flicker and
dim one by one, as if something drains their light. Tiny round doors of the
mushroom houses slam shut in sequence, left to right. From the black canopy,
two glowing orange eyes open. MANANANGGAL drops into frame in a steep dive,
wings snapping open with a heavy overshoot, purple mist trailing in curling
ribbons, her long tongue flicking toward the camera.

Shot 2 (5–10s): Smash-cut to a heroic low-angle close shot on KING standing on
the cap of the tallest mushroom, cape fluttering with overlapping action. Micro-
acting: one eyebrow lifts, his beard bristles, he adjusts his tilted crown with
a deadpan push of one finger. Big anticipation: he crouches deep, the mushroom
cap squashes under him like a trampoline, then he launches with full stretch,
twig sword raised, spinning once in the air. The camera whips up to follow him.

Shot 3 (10–15s): Cut on action to a wide shot. KING bonks MANANANGGAL squarely on
the head with the twig sword; impact frame with a bright flash and 3 frames of
hit-stop. Her eyes cross, her wings crumple, and she bursts into a harmless,
cartoon puff of purple mist and sparkles that swirls away into the trees. KING
lands in a heroic three-point pose, the mud squashing around his boots, crown
bouncing and settling. The glowing mushrooms relight in a wave; the tiny doors
fly open and dozens of little Duwende mushroom gnomes pour out, jumping and
cheering around him. Final beat: KING raises the twig sword in triumph as the
camera booms up into a grand low-angle hero shot, fireflies swirling.

Family-friendly cartoon tone: no blood, no gore, the villain is defeated by a
comic bonk and vanishes in sparkles.

NO BACKGROUND MUSIC. No speech. The soundscape opens in humid jungle dusk:
crickets, dripping leaves, distant frogs. Each mushroom dimming gives a soft
electric fizz; the tiny doors slam with wooden clicks. A leathery wing-snap and
a shrill, cartoonish screech as MANANANGGAL dives, with a low whooshing purple
mist. A springy, rubbery boing as the mushroom cap launches KING, then an airy
spin whoosh. The bonk lands with a hollow wooden thwack, a split second of
silence during hit-stop, then a bright magical poof and twinkling sparkles. KING
lands with a wet mud squelch and a tiny metallic crown wobble. The scene closes
on a swelling chorus of tiny, squeaky gnome cheers over the jungle ambience.
